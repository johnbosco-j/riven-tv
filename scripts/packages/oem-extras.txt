# Thomson (SPPL / Skyworth-built, MediaTek) Google TV extras.
# Adapted from CleanYourTV's tested list for a Thomson SPPL 4K Google TV (SW6H), MIT licence,
# https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv/tree/main/examples/thomson-sppl-4k-sw6h
# Only packages actually present and enabled on your TV are touched. Run `./riven.sh scan` first.
#
# NEVER add: com.smartdevice.livetv (Live TV + HDMI), com.mediatek.tvinput, com.mediatek.tv.service,
# com.mediatek.android.tv.globalkeyoverlay (hot keys), com.smartdevice.duplebluetooth /
# bleconnectservice (remote pairing), anything with settings, keyboard, factory, ota or update.
